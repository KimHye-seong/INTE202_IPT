Polytechnic University of the Philippines

School Description

The Polytechnic University of the Philippines (PUP) is a public, non-sectarian, non-profit state university. It became the Polytechnic University of the Philippines through Presidential Decree No. 1341, issued on April 1, 1978. With more than twenty campuses across the country, PUP makes quality and accessible education available to many students and aims to produce skilled, service-minded professionals for nation building.
