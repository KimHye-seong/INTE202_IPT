Polytechnic University of the Philippines

Vision

A Leading Comprehensive Polytechnic University in Asia

Mission

Advance an inclusive, equitable, and globally relevant polytechnic education towards national development.

Philosophy

As a State University, the Polytechnic University of the Philippines believes that education is an instrument for the development of the citizenry and for the enhancement of nation building. It believes that the meaningful growth and transformation of the country are best achieved in an atmosphere of brotherhood, peace, freedom, justice and 36 a nationalist-oriented education imbued with the spirit of humanist internationalism.

  3. Enabling and Productive Learning Environment

  8. Synergistic, Productive, Strategic Networks and Partnerships
  9. Active and Sustained Stakeholders' Engagement
